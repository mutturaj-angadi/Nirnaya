# Dataset catalog and preparation policy

Nirnaya separates benchmark inputs by provenance. None of the bundled refinery examples contain MRPL operational data. The flagship is named **MRPL-inspired synthetic scenario** solely to describe its domain.

## Categories

| Category | Contents | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| A2 · NOC-10K synthetic industrial-style optimization | 10,000 deterministic LPs across 12 structural families | Generated by `scripts/generate_noc10k.py`; metadata/hash manifest at `datasets/noc10k/manifest.jsonl.gz`; no plant or MRPL data. The requested family allocation summed to 9,750, so 250 cases were added to synthetic industrial-style production/resource allocation to meet the explicit 10,000 total. See `docs/NOC10K.md`. |
| A · Synthetic validation | Dense, sparse, highly sparse, degenerate, ill-scaled, assignment, transportation, and diagnostic status LPs | Generated by `benchmarks/generate_synthetic_suite.py` with global seed `20260928` and per-family seeds recorded in each model's metadata. The assignment and transportation families are exact deterministic constructions. |
| B · Industrial-style synthetic | Refinery blending, crude allocation, multi-period production planning, resource allocation, transport, and the flagship decision case | Nirnaya-authored illustrative assumptions. Not plant telemetry, an MRPL planning model, or an approved operational recommendation. The flagship model's generator documents yields, crude availability/cost, market limits, quality constraints, and omitted factors. |
| C · Standard optimization references | External LP/MIP collections | No third-party instances are currently bundled. The [Netlib LP data index](https://www.netlib.org/lp/data/) lists MPS test problems; file-specific provenance and redistribution terms must be checked before adding any instance. Its [general Netlib license](https://www.netlib.org/math/license.html) does not, by itself, establish the rights for every contributed dataset. [MIPLIB](https://miplib.zib.de/) is primarily mixed-integer and therefore outside the current continuous-LP solver scope. |

## Machine-readable sources

- [Dataset manifest](../benchmarks/manifest.json) records dataset ID, source, license declaration, `synthetic`/`public` classification, dimensions, nonzeros, assumptions, preprocessing, scenario names, and latest actually measured solver/reference fields. The 18 bundled entries are synthetic; external public collections are candidates only and are not bundled.
- [Preparation audit](../benchmarks/datasets/preparation_audit.json) is generated by `python benchmarks/audit_datasets.py`. It is read-only: it reports duplicate rows/names, non-finite data, impossible bounds, zero/near-zero/large coefficients, and missing fields. It never rewrites an input model.
- Synthetic LP files are generated deterministically into `benchmarks/datasets/synthetic/` by `python benchmarks/generate_synthetic_suite.py`.
- The flagship model is generated into both `examples/flagship/refinery_decision_demo.json` and the UI's local asset directory with `python examples/flagship/generate_refinery_demo.py`.

## Preparation and cleaning

No supplied or synthetic dataset is silently cleaned. For every audited model, the audit records original/retained/removed rows and columns; current policy sets removed counts to zero and transformations to an empty list. A duplicate row in `degenerate_duplicate_rows` is an intentional degeneracy/presolve stress feature and is retained. Other exact duplicate rows are flagged for review, not dropped. Coefficients close to zero or unusually large are reported, not thresholded. Units are documented per dataset, but automated dimensional consistency checking is not implemented; the audit says this explicitly.

The model validator separately rejects malformed/non-finite values and impossible bounds before solving. An invalid dataset is not repaired or replaced with a successful instance.

## Flagship assumption ledger

`examples/flagship/refinery_decision_demo.json` is an illustrative LP with crude slate variables, yield-linked intermediate streams, two gasoline grades, domestic/export shipment variables, throughput/loading capacities, minimum deliveries, and linear RON/RVP blend constraints. Its objective uses assumed market contribution less crude acquisition costs. Taxes, energy balance, emissions, nonlinear blend interactions, contracts, outages, uncertainty, and real plant operating rules are omitted. The dataset metadata and generator are the complete assumption ledger; see `docs/DEMO.md` for the judge workflow.
